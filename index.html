Badminton is a sport!

Badminton is a racket sport played by either two opposing players (singles) or two opposing pairs (doubles).

The objective is simple: hit the shuttlecock over the net and land it inside your opponent's court.

Despite its simple appearance, badminton requires speed, precision, coordination, and strategy.

Whether played casually with friends or competitively at an international level, badminton is a sport that combines fun and physical challenge.

Some fun facts about badminton:

1. Badminton is surprising fast: A badminton smash has been recorded at over 500 km/h in professional competitions and speed tests.

2. Shuttlecocks don't fly like balls: Their aerodynamic design makes them slow down rapidly after being hit, which creates badminton's distinctive playing style.
